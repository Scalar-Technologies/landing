Privacy Policy

Study Manager · Last updated November 22, 2026

Welcome to Study Manager (“we,” “our,” “us”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application (the “App”). By using the App, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

a. Information You Provide to Us

  • Feedback and Support: If you contact us for support or provide feedback, we collect the information you provide.
  • Content for AI Features: If you use the Smart Assistant or the Schedule Scanner, we process the content described in Section 3.

b. Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: We collect information about how you interact with the App, including features you use, session duration, and error reports.
  • Device Information: We collect information about your device, such as its model, operating system, and unique identifiers.

c. Payment Information

Payments for subscriptions are processed through Apple’s in-app purchase system, and we do not store or process your payment information directly.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and improve the App’s functionality and features.
  • Ensure compliance with legal and regulatory requirements.

3. AI Features

Study Manager Plus includes two features powered by artificial intelligence. Both can be avoided or turned off, and the rest of the App works without them.

a. Smart Assistant

The Smart Assistant gives you short study advice based on your tasks. To generate it, the App prepares a summary that includes today’s date, the names, courses and deadlines of your active tasks, the first 80 characters of their descriptions, the names and courses of your completed tasks, and the language of the App.

For Study Manager Plus subscribers, the Smart Assistant is turned on by default and uses the Remote model. In Settings → Smart Assistant you can choose the AI model:

  • Local (Apple Intelligence): On supported devices, the advice is generated on your device by Apple’s on-device model. The summary does not leave your device.
  • Remote: The summary is sent to our server and then to our AI provider, which generates the advice.

You can turn the Smart Assistant off in the same place at any time. While it is off, the App does not prepare or send the summary.

b. Schedule Scanner

The Schedule Scanner only runs when you open it and confirm a scan; nothing is sent before that. When you scan a schedule, the photo or image you choose, cropped to the area you select, is sent to our server and then to our AI provider, which reads it and returns the courses and lessons it finds. Please avoid scanning images that contain information you do not want to share. Results may contain inaccuracies, so review them before saving.

c. How AI Requests Are Processed

  • Providers: Remote requests are sent from our server to OpenRouter, which passes them to a model operated by OpenAI. These providers process the content only to generate a response and handle it under their own privacy policies.
  • Identifier: Each request includes the identifier of your App Store subscription transaction, which we use to verify your subscription and apply daily usage limits. It does not include your name, email address, or Apple Account.
  • Storage: We do not save your scanned images or task summaries on our server. To avoid repeating identical requests, the server keeps the most recent advice for your subscription in memory for up to 24 hours. It is never written to disk.
  • No training: We do not use your content to train AI models.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. However, we may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: Third-party vendors that help us operate the App (e.g., analytics, and the AI providers described in Section 3).
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of the transaction.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information as long as necessary to provide the App’s services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at support@studystudy.study.

8. Children’s Privacy
